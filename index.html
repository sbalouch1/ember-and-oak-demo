Our story

One oven. Oak fire. No gas, no shortcuts.

Ember & Oak began in 2019 with a single wood-burning hearth and a stubborn idea: that fire is the oldest recipe there is. Our menu changes with the seasons and the farms we work with across Illinois and Wisconsin.
